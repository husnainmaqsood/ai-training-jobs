How it works

  1. 1. Find a job you're eligible for

    Search by skill, category, platform, pay and — most importantly — your country. Each listing is labelled Worldwide (verified), specific countries or eligibility not verified. Listings show a "last verified" date, and expired or broken listings are removed.

  2. 2. Upload your master CV once

    Upload a PDF or DOCX, or paste text. We keep your original file untouched and extract its text so you can check it.

  3. 3. Tailor it to the job

    The AI compares the job's requirements to your actual experience, then rewrites your summary, skills and bullet points to emphasise what's relevant. It references your existing roles by ID and cannot change employers, titles, dates or degrees. A second, non-AI fact check rejects any new numbers, skills or technologies that aren't in your CV — and tells you what it rejected.

  4. 4. Review the fit analysis

    See which requirements your CV supports (with quotes from your CV as evidence), which are partial, and which are missing. The coverage estimate is a simple weighted count — it is not an employer ATS score or a prediction of being hired.

  5. 5. Edit, download, apply, track

    Edit any wording, download an ATS-friendly PDF or DOCX, apply on the employer's official site, and record progress in your tracker.
