FAQ

Straight answers

Are you the employer?
No. You apply on the company's own site and they run selection, contracts and pay. We're an independent directory.
Do I need AI experience?
Usually not. Companies are buying expertise in your field; familiarity with AI tools helps but is rarely required.
What does it pay?
It varies by company, field and seniority. Where a rate is published we show it on the listing{% if stats.total %} — {{ stats.with_rate|percent_of:stats.total }}% of current listings have one{% endif %}.
Is there a fee to apply?
Never. Treat any request for payment in exchange for a job as a warning sign.
Are the listings checked?
Each listing shows when it was last verified. Closed roles are removed from the directory.
